About

A speedrunning leaderboard for Portal 2 beta builds. Browse the builds and categories on the home page, or sign in with Discord to submit a run.

Check the category rules before submitting. Runs are reviewed by moderators before appearing on the leaderboard.

Join the "Portal Series Beta Research" Discord server to discuss strats, runs and glitches!
